# vocabulario

1. **yo** | This word means "I" and is the first word in Spanish that you'll have to learn.  It's a big word in a small package, and it bends in odd ways —many times we don't even write ir or say it in regular texts and speech, for it has some special attributes, but it's you we're talking about! So please, memorize the word _yo_. How do you pronounce it? Just try to say "John" without the letters h and n… Jo? 
2. **tú** | The second word means "you" —the one that's in front of you, the one who isn't you… See that extra line on the letter _u_? It's an accent. It's a way we have to differentiate some words from others and to indicate that such letter —the accented one— is spoken with extra force — **little** extra force… How do we pronounce "tú"? Try to say "Tupac"   
3. **y** | This word means "and", like in "you and I", or "me and you"… And it's a fun one to work with. We use it when we coordinate elements or sentences… Like in English. We can make lists too… But we hide it in long ones…

   And we can make our first sentence in Spanish:

   _Tú y yo_. —meaning "you and I". In spanish, if we write elegantly, we place the second –or third— person in front of the first person… It's respect. If we are entering anywhere, we let in the others first. The same with this two pronouns —that is what they are, by the way: pronouns…

4. **él** | This word is intense.
